Billing Ethics

Understanding How IP Costs Are Structured

Every intellectual property application entails two separate financial components: legal prosecution fees and official government statutory fees.

Our Billing Pledge

No Surprises, No Retainer Runaway

Unlike conventional billable-hour firms, LexVanguard itemizes every filing fee down to the penny. You review statutory registry receipts alongside our flat legal drafting rates before any filing is submitted.

1. Professional Legal Fees

These cover LexVanguard's substantive legal and technical labor: conducting conflict clearance searches, preparing opinion letters, drafting Nice classifications or patent claims, filing paperwork, and tracking docket deadlines.

  • Fixed, agreed-upon pricing prior to engagement
  • No surprise hourly billing for standard applications
  • Covers non-final procedural office actions

2. Government / Statutory Fees

Mandatory official processing fees paid directly to national IP offices (e.g., USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO, WIPO). These vary based on jurisdiction, entity size (Micro/Small/Large entity for patents), and the number of international classes.

  • Set strictly by federal statute, not law firms
  • Reimbursed at actual cost with zero markup
  • Dependent on Nice class count and applicant size
