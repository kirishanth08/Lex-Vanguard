The Roadmap

The Complete IP Prosecution Lifecycle

Each stage is engineered to eliminate procedural errors, safeguard filing dates, and ensure defensible registrations across global registries.

Predictable Methodology

From Concept to Grant: Transparent, Audited Milestones

Navigating bureaucratic government trademark and patent registries shouldn't feel like a black box. We provide transparent milestones, real-time docket updates, and realistic timelines across USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO, and WIPO.

Jurisdictional Benchmarks Document Readiness Checklist
1

Stage 1: Intake & Strategy Alignment

Days 1–3

We review your commercial assets, launch milestones, target consumer markets, and prior filings. An IP attorney determines whether a trademark, provisional patent, or copyright deposit provides optimal protection.

2

Stage 2: Clearance Search & Risk Audit

Days 4–7

Our team conducts comprehensive searches across federal databases, international treaty registers, and common-law commerce channels. You receive an attorney opinion letter detailing potential likelihood-of-confusion or prior art risks.

3

Stage 3: Legal Drafting & Technical Claim Formulation

Days 8–14

We draft formal Nice Class specifications for trademarks or independent/dependent claims and patent illustrations. The application package is delivered to you for thorough final review and authorization.

4

Stage 4: Official Electronic Submission

Day 15

We file directly into the designated government registry (USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO, WIPO). Official filing receipts, official serial numbers, and statutory priority timestamps are issued immediately and securely vaulted.

5

Stage 5: Government Examination & Office Action Prosecution

Months 3–12*

The government examining attorney inspects the filing. If procedural queries or substantive refusals arise, LexVanguard prepares legal arguments, amends descriptions, or submits supplemental specimens.

6

Stage 6: Certificate Grant & Post-Registration Monitoring

Ongoing

Following the publication period without opposition, the official Certificate of Registration is issued. We enroll your mark or patent into our docket monitoring system to watch for infringers and manage maintenance renewals.

Realistic Jurisdictional Timelines & Benchmarks

We never make false promises or pretend government bureaucracies operate overnight. Below are actual empirical timelines across major intellectual property registries.

Docketing Discipline

Statutory Deadlines Are Non-Negotiable

Missing a 3-month USPTO office action response or a 30-day opposition window results in statutory abandonment. Our automated dual-calendar docketing tracks every statutory milestone across global jurisdictions.

United States USPTO
10–14 Months (TM)
18–30 Months (Patent)

First Office Action: ~8.5 months from filing date.

Publication Period: 30 days in the Official Gazette before issuance of notice of allowance.

European Union EUIPO
4–6 Months (TM)
Fast Track: 3 Weeks

First Office Action: ~4 weeks via Fast Track filing.

Opposition Window: 3 months for 27 member states following publication.

United Kingdom UKIPO
3–5 Months (TM)
Direct: 1–2 Weeks

First Office Action: ~10 to 15 business days.

Opposition Window: 2 months (extendable by 1 month upon formal request).
