Comprehensive Scope

The Foundation of Commercial Brand Ownership

A registered trademark transforms your brand identity from an unprotected marketing concept into an enforceable legal monopoly.

Without formal registration with the USPTO, EUIPO, or UKIPO, your business relies solely on fragile "common law" geographic rights. Federal registration confers a nationwide legal presumption of ownership, blocking competitors from adopting confusingly similar marks across all 50 states and international treaty partner nations.

Section 1(a) — Use in Commerce

Section 1(b) — Intent-to-Use (ITU)

Who Requires Comprehensive Trademark Filing?

Federal trademark protection is essential for any enterprise investing capital in customer acquisition and brand equity.

E-Commerce & Amazon

Amazon Brand Registry 2.0 requires an active or pending registered trademark. Unlock Project Zero counterfeit protections and A+ Content.

Statutory Exclusive Rights

Enforceable legal protections and judicial remedies granted by official government registration:

Federal Court Jurisdiction & Treble Damages

Direct standing to sue infringers in U.S. Federal Court with eligibility to recover infringer profits and triple statutory damages.

Customs & Border Protection (CBP) Recordation

Record your registration with border authorities to intercept and seize counterfeit imported merchandise before reaching domestic markets.

International Treaty Priority (Madrid Protocol)

Use your home application to obtain international trademark protection in up to 130 countries via a single WIPO filing.

Client Preparation Checklist

To expedite your trademark filing, our team will review the following details during onboarding:

1. Mark Representation Standard character wordmark, or high-resolution vector/PNG logo file.
2. Owner Legal Entity Legal entity name, state of incorporation (e.g., Delaware LLC), or individual owner citizenship.
3. Goods / Services List Clear explanation of what products or commercial services are sold under the mark.
4. Specimen of Use (For 1(a) filings) Unedited photos of labels, packaging, product tags, or live checkout screenshots showing the mark with price.
Filing Journey

The Trademark Registration Lifecycle

Understanding each statutory milestone from clearance search to official registration certificate.

1

Stage 1: Clearance Search & Attorney Opinion

Stage 1

We conduct an in-depth audit across federal databases, state registries, common law domains, and trademark gazettes to flag potential likelihood-of-confusion conflicts.

2

Stage 2: Application Drafting & Class Specification

Stage 2

Drafting precise Nice Class descriptions to ensure maximum statutory coverage without overbreadth rejections. Client approves draft prior to docketing.

3

Stage 3: Official Electronic Filing & Serial Number

Stage 3

Direct electronic submission through the national registry portal. Serial number and official filing receipt are issued immediately, locking your priority date.

4

Stage 4: Government Examining Attorney Review

Stage 4

The application is assigned to an examining attorney. If procedural queries arise, LexVanguard prepares and files formal legal responses.

5

Stage 5: Publication for Opposition & Certificate Issuance

Stage 5

The mark is published in the Official Gazette for a 30-day public opposition window. Once cleared, the official Certificate of Registration is issued.
